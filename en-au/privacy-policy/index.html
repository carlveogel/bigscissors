Privacy policy

What we collect when you use this site, why we collect it, and what you can do about it.

Effective 19 November 2026

The short version

We collect as little as we can. We have no user accounts and take no payments. Most of what we see is anonymous traffic data used to understand which pages are useful.

What we collect

  • Things you send us, such as your name and email address if you contact us.
  • Usage data collected automatically: browser and device type, referring page, pages viewed and similar statistics.

We don't knowingly collect sensitive information, so please don't send it to us.

How we use it

To run and improve the site, answer messages, keep the site secure and understand which content readers find useful.

Cookies and analytics

We and our analytics providers may use cookies to measure traffic and remember preferences. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site will still work, though some features may not.

Affiliate links and other sites

Some links take you to casino operators, and we may earn a commission if you sign up. Once you leave this site, the other site's own privacy policy applies, and we aren't responsible for how it handles your data.

How long we keep it

Only as long as we need it: to answer you, run the site, meet legal obligations or resolve a dispute. After that it's deleted or anonymised.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect what we hold. No internet transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can't promise absolute protection.

Your choices

You can turn off cookies, choose not to contact us, or ask what information we hold about you. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under privacy law, including the Australian Privacy Principles.

Under-18s

This site is for adults only. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here with a new effective date.
