Method

How a casino earns its score

We open a real account, fund it with Australian dollars, play, and cash out. Six things decide the final number.

  1. Getting paid

    We time real withdrawals by every method offered. Crypto under two hours and fiat under a day score top marks; anything past 48 hours loses points.

  2. Bonus maths

    We work out what an offer is worth after wagering, stake caps, game weighting and cashout limits, rather than taking the headline figure.

  3. Licence and security

    We confirm the offshore licence with the issuing body, check encryption and look for a history of complaints or unpaid winnings.

  4. Game quality

    We look at who supplies the games, whether RTPs are published, and whether in-house titles can be verified as provably fair.

  5. Phone performance

    Every site is played on iPhone and Android browsers, through the full deposit, play and withdrawal flow.

  6. Player protection

    Deposit limits, time-outs and self-exclusion need to be easy to find and actually work. Sites without them don't make the list.

Bonus guide

Casino bonuses, explained properly

The headline figure is the least useful part of an offer. Here's what the common types mean and how to tell what one is worth.

Deposit match
The casino adds a percentage of your deposit as bonus funds. 100% up to AU$1,000 turns a AU$500 deposit into AU$1,000 to play with.
Free spins
Spins on chosen pokies. Winnings usually arrive as bonus money with their own wagering requirement.
Cash gift / no-wager bonus
Bonus credit you can withdraw without a playthrough. Rare, and usually smaller, but worth more than it looks.
Cashback
A share of your net losses returned over a set period, either as cash or as bonus money.
Reloads
Smaller match offers for existing players, often on weekends or tied to a specific day.
VIP schemes
Points earned on every bet, traded for cashback, quicker withdrawals or a personal account manager.

Worked example: what wagering really costs

If the requirement applies to deposit plus bonus, those totals double. Always check which one the terms use.

Five things to check before you opt in

  • Playthrough. Around 35x is reasonable. Above 50x, the bonus is rarely worth taking.
  • Game weighting. Pokies usually count 100% towards wagering; live and table games often count 5–20%, or nothing at all.
  • Stake cap. Most offers limit bets to around AU$5–10 while a bonus is active. Going over can void your winnings.
  • Cashout limit. Some bonuses cap what you can withdraw from bonus winnings.
  • Time limit. Expect 7 to 30 days to finish wagering before the bonus disappears.

The law

What Australian law says about online casinos

The Interactive Gambling Act 2001 bans operators from offering real-money online casino games, including pokies, roulette, blackjack and similar games, to people in Australia. That applies to companies based overseas too. Only online sports and race wagering can be licensed here.

The Act targets operators, not players, so using an offshore casino is not a crime for you. It does leave you without the protections that come with a local licence:

  • No Australian regulator or ombudsman can step in if a casino refuses to pay.
  • ACMA regularly blocks offshore casino sites, so a site you use today may become unreachable.
  • BetStop, the national self-exclusion register, does not cover these sites.
  • Gambling winnings are generally not taxed for recreational players.

FAQ

Common questions

Is it legal to play at online casinos from Australia?

The Interactive Gambling Act 2001 makes it illegal for any operator, local or overseas, to offer online casino games such as pokies, roulette and blackjack to people in Australia. Playing is not an offence for you as an individual, but none of these sites hold an Australian licence, ACMA blocks many of them, and you have no Australian regulator to turn to if a withdrawal is refused.

Which bonus is the best value?

Goldenbet's AU$100 cash on each of your first three deposits, because it has no wagering attached. If you want the biggest headline number instead, Lucky Vibe's AU$5,000 plus 300 spins is the largest in our top five.

What does 40x wagering actually mean?

You have to bet 40 times the bonus amount before bonus money and its winnings can be withdrawn. A AU$100 bonus at 40x means AU$4,000 in total bets. Our bonus section works through a full example.

Do I pay tax on gambling winnings?

For recreational players, generally no. The ATO does not usually treat gambling winnings as income unless gambling is run as a business. If you're unsure about your situation, check with a tax adviser.

Can I use BetStop to block these casinos?

No. BetStop only covers Australian-licensed online and phone wagering services. To block offshore casinos, use the account limits on each site, ask your bank to block gambling transactions, and install blocking software.

How do I check whether a gambling site is licensed in Australia?

Search ACMA's register of licensed interactive wagering providers. If a site offering online pokies or table games is accepting Australians, it is not licensed here.
