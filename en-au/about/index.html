How We Evaluate Online Casinos

Every casino is scored across six critical metrics. Here is exactly what we test and why it matters.

01

Payout Speed

What we test: We request a real withdrawal via each available payment method — crypto, bank transfer, and debit card — and record the time from request to funds available.

Why it matters: Slow payouts are the most common player complaint. A casino can have great games and bonuses, but if it holds your money for a week, the experience is worthless.

How we measure: Stopwatch timing from withdrawal confirmation to funds clearing. Under 2 hours for crypto, under 24 hours for fiat = top score.

02

Fairness

What we test: We verify RTP disclosures against actual session results, check for provably fair certification on in-house games, and review terms for hidden restrictions.

Why it matters: Players need to know the games are not rigged and bonus terms are transparent. Hidden clauses and unfair playthrough requirements erode trust.

How we measure: Cross-reference stated RTP with third-party audit reports. Review bonus terms line by line. Flag any wagering requirements above 50x or unfair max bet rules.

03

Mobile Stability

What we test: We play on iOS Safari, Chrome for Android, and tablet browsers. We test game load times, lobby navigation, account management, and deposit/withdrawal flows.

Why it matters: Over 70% of Australian players use mobile devices. A casino that works poorly on mobile effectively locks out most of its audience.

How we measure: Load time benchmarks, crash frequency, UI responsiveness. Any casino with game-breaking mobile bugs is disqualified from top rankings.

04

Bonus Value

What we test: We calculate real bonus value by factoring in wagering requirements, game contribution rates, max bet limits, withdrawal caps, and expiry periods.

Why it matters: A AU$10,000 bonus with 60x wagering on 10% contribution slots is worse than a AU$1,000 bonus with 35x wagering on 100% contribution slots. Headline numbers lie.

How we measure: We run a standardised value formula: (bonus amount / wagering requirement) x weighted game contribution = effective value. Then adjust for withdrawal caps and expiry.

05

Security Checks

What we test: We verify SSL encryption, licensing validity with the issuing authority, KYC/AML procedures, data protection policies, and the casino's history of security incidents.

Why it matters: Australian players are sending money to offshore platforms. Without proper security, personal data and funds are at risk.

How we measure: SSL certificate validation through security tools. License verification against Curaçao eGaming and MGA registries. Background check for past breaches or complaints.

06

Availability in Australia

What we test: We confirm that the casino accepts Australian players, supports AUD (or crypto with clear conversion), offers Australian-friendly deposit methods, and does not block Australian IPs.

Why it matters: Many casinos claim to accept Australians but block registration from Australian IP addresses or exclude AUD as a currency option.

How we measure: Full registration and deposit test from an Australian IP address. Confirmation of AUD support or fair crypto conversion. Verification that withdrawal methods work for Australian bank accounts.
