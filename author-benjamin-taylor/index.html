His checklist

  1. Open an account

    Registers as a normal player and notes verification demands and how clear the terms are.

  2. Deposit and claim

    Funds the account, claims the offer and confirms that the cashier matches the published terms.

  3. Play a full session

    Pokies, live tables, crash and table games, checking speed, stability and RTP disclosure.

  4. Cash out

    Requests a withdrawal and records the timing, fees and any extra checks along the way.

Standards

Benjamin doesn't score a bonus on its size. A huge package with steep wagering or a tight cashout cap can rank below a small, clean one.

Responsible gambling tools count towards the score: limits, self-exclusion, clear age checks and links to support. Affiliate relationships play no part in his assessments.
